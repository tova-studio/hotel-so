Overview

Centrally located hotels for today’s business and leisure travellers.

Grand Hotels International owns and operates centrally-located hotels in Australia, New Zealand, Malaysia, Singapore and China, offering attractive accommodation for today’s contemporary business and leisure travellers.

In Australia and New Zealand, ten 4–4½ star hotels are marketed under the renowned Hotel Grand Chancellor brand. As well as providing well-appointed accommodation, the hotels have extensive conference and event facilities, with five venues catering for over 400 delegates.

The Australian properties are located in the cities of Brisbane, Melbourne, Adelaide, Hobart, Launceston and Townsville. In New Zealand, hotels are located in the Central Business Districts of Wellington and Auckland.

Throughout Malaysia, from Langkawi to Kuching, six Hotel Grand Continental and Hotel Grand Crystal properties offer a range of options for travellers. In China, Hotel Grand Chancellor Sihui is situated in Jade City, close to Sihui, Dong Cheng City Government Office, providing corporate guests with large conference and restaurant facilities.

Aligning with our dedication to progression and development, Hotel Chancellor@Orchard and Hotel Grand Central are the group’s two newest properties located on Orchard Road in central Singapore.

Our history

How Hotel Grand Central Limited grew into Australia and New Zealand.

  1. Early 1990s

    Hotel Grand Central Limited expanded its hotel and commercial property interest into Australia and New Zealand, purchasing commercial property in Sydney, Australia and Wellington, New Zealand.

  2. 1995

    Further expansion occurred with the purchase of several hotel properties in Australia and New Zealand. At this point, Grand Central Management Limited was established to manage the various hotel and commercial property interests.
